Methodology · Daily CO₂ is derived from NLDC SCADA 15-min generation data, not directly measured. Thermal CO₂ = Thermal (MU) × 950 tCO₂/GWh (CEA coal EF 2023-24). Gas CO₂ = Gas (MU) × 450 tCO₂/GWh (IPCC). Avoided emissions = (Solar + Wind) × 950 tCO₂/GWh vs coal displacement baseline. Grid EF = Total CO₂ ÷ Total generation (MWh). Rooftop / distributed RE (~227 GW installed) excluded per CEA DGR scope. Sources: NLDC PSP · Grid-India · CEA CO₂ Baseline Database 2023-24.
